**CarTracker** is an Android app for your own [Traccar](https://www.traccar.org) server. It shows where your car is, alerts you the moment something happens, keeps your trips and controls the tracker, with no Google keys, no Firebase and no cloud other than your own server.

## What it needs

- Android 8.0 or newer
- A Traccar server, version 5 or newer (6 recommended), reachable over HTTPS. Plain http works only on your home network
- A Traccar account with your car linked to it

The screenshots on this wiki come from the app itself, signed in to a demo server with a made-up car in Dublin.
