# Widget and quick settings

## Home-screen widget

Long-press your home screen → **Widgets** → **CarTracker**, and drag it where you want it.

It shows the car you have chosen in the app:

- the car's pin, name and street
- **Parked 2 h 5 m**, or **Driving · 48 km/h**, or **Offline**
- the battery voltage, orange when it's below your [warning level](Battery#low-battery-warning)
- when it last reported

Tap it to open the app. With [live alerts](Alerts#live-alerts) on, it updates with every position and the parked time moves on every minute. With live alerts off, it updates every 15 to 30 minutes.

## Quick settings tile

Pull down the notification shade twice, tap the **pencil** (edit), and drag **Car alarm** into your tiles.

- The tile shows the car's name and whether the alarm is **on** or **off**.
- Tap it to turn the alarm on or off. If the phone is locked, it asks you to unlock first.
- Like in the app, it changes only once the tracker confirms, and says **Waiting for the tracker…** until then. With alerts for **Tracker replies** on, a notification confirms it too.
- It's greyed out while the car is offline, or if the tracker can't arm from the app.
