# Places

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/CarTracker/images/place-edit.png" width="270" align="right" alt="Editing a place">

Places are areas like home or work. The app alerts you when the car arrives at or leaves one, and alerts show places by name.

## Adding a place

Park the car where you want the place, then go to **More → Places → Add a place where … is now**.

- **Name**: Home, Work, Gym…
- **Size**: how far around the car the place reaches, from 50 m to 2 km.
- **Icon**: home, work, pin, school, shop, parking, heart or star.

## Changing or removing one

- **Tap a place** to change its name, size or icon.
- **The bin** next to it removes it.

Places are saved on your Traccar server as geofences, so they work in Traccar's own apps too. Places drawn in Traccar as shapes other than a circle show in the list, but can only be changed in Traccar.

For place alerts, make sure **Arriving and leaving places** is on under [Which alerts to get](Alerts#which-alerts-to-get).
