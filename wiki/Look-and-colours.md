# Look and colours

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/CarTracker/images/more.png" width="270" align="right" alt="Your car's look">

Everything that has a colour can be any colour you like.

## Your car's pin

Under **More → Your car**:

- **Name**: the car's name, also changed on the server.
- **Vehicle**: hatchback, estate, small car, SUV, van, pickup, truck or motorbike.
- **Car colour**: the car's paint. Glass, lights and tyres stay as they are.
- **Glow colour**: the ring and the point around the car.

Each car has its own look.

<br clear="right">

## Picking a colour

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/bornaradusin/CarTracker/images/colour-picker.png" width="270" align="right" alt="Picking a colour">

Every colour setting opens the same picker:

- **R, G and B sliders**, with a live preview
- **Hex**: type a colour code, like `#1D5BD8`
- **Default**: back to the original colour

Tap **Save** to keep it, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was.

<br clear="right">

## The app

Under **More → Theme**:

- **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow phone**
- **App colour**: buttons, switches, highlights and routes. Light colours get a darker outline so they stay readable.
